# Color Legend and Keyboard Map

## The seven colors

| Note | Color | Hex | Shape | Sticker idea |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C | Red | `#D32F2F` | circle | red dot |
| D | Orange | `#F57C00` | triangle | orange dot |
| E | Yellow | `#FBC02D` | star | yellow dot (black outline) |
| F | Green | `#388E3C` | square | green dot |
| G | Blue | `#1976D2` | diamond | blue dot |
| A | Purple | `#7B1FA2` | heart | purple dot |
| B | Pink | `#C2185B` | hexagon | pink dot |

Print the wall chart and the small reference cards from
`printables/piano-printables.html`, pages 1–3.

## The home octave

Use the C in the middle of the keyboard (middle C). Place one sticker on the
front edge of each white key:

```
 key:   C   D   E   F   G   A   B   C
color: red org yel grn blu pur pnk red
finger RH: 1  2  3  4  5  -   -   -
finger LH: 5  4  3  2  1  -   -   -
```

The first five notes, C–G, are the "five-finger position." The child's right
thumb rests on red C and the pinky on blue G. Every early song stays inside
these five keys.

```
        ┌───┬───┬───┬───┬───┐
 right  │ 1 │ 2 │ 3 │ 4 │ 5 │
 hand   │ C │ D │ E │ F │ G │
        │red│org│yel│grn│blu│
        └───┴───┴───┴───┴───┘
             thumb → pinky
```

For the left hand the numbers mirror: pinky (5) on C, thumb (1) on G.

## Why these colors

The palette follows common Boomwhacker and desk-bell sets. That means the same
chart works with colored instruments in a group class, and replacement
stickers are easy to find.

**Contrast warning.** Yellow on white paper is hard to see. Always give the
yellow E a black outline, print on cream paper rather than bright white, or
use a deeper amber. Never write yellow text on white.

## Accessibility: color is never the only cue

Roughly 1 in 12 boys has some color vision deficiency. Every chart in this
curriculum therefore carries at least two cues:

- the color itself,
- the letter name,
- a shape marker or a distinct border.

When teaching, say **"red circle C"** rather than "red." The redundant naming
costs nothing and keeps the system open to every child.

A letter-only version of the first songs is on page 6 of the printables, and a
shape-only version is on page 7. Letter-only sequences for every song are in
`song-charts/all-songs.md`.

## Extending the map

Once the child knows C–B in the home octave:

- add a second set of stickers one octave higher;
- move the whole set to G for a transposition game;
- leave the stickers off for one song and use the chart only.

Remove stickers gradually after lesson 9 so the child begins to use the black
key groups (two black keys, three black keys) as landmarks.
