Every chart also carries letter names and shape markers, so color is never the only cue. Say “red circle C.”

Start here

You need a piano or keyboard, a few colored stickers, and five minutes of practice a day.

  1. Print the pack.Use A4, portrait, 100% scale, in color. Cut out the seven color cards.
  2. Sticker the keys.Place one dot on C–B in the middle octave. Start with red C.
  3. Teach Lesson 1.No note names, no finger numbers — just gentle sounds and high/low.
  4. Practise five minutes a day.Short and happy beats long and tired.
