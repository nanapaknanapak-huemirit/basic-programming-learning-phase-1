# Lesson 1 — Hello, Piano!

**Unit 1: Explore** · 30 minutes · First lesson

**You need:** piano, red C sticker, stuffed animal, soft ball, color cards
(page 3 of the printables).

## I can…

- sit at the piano with my feet supported;
- make ten gentle "raindrop" touches;
- show high and low sounds;
- find red C.

## At a glance

| Min | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Hello song and name game |
| 3–7 | Body warm-up and piano posture |
| 7–12 | High and low game |
| 12–19 | Exploring the keys gently |
| 19–26 | Raindrops, storms, and sunshine |
| 26–28 | Play for the stuffed animal |
| 28–30 | Home card |

## 1. Hello song (3 min)

Sing on two notes, G and E: "Hel-lo, [name], hel-lo!" Child echoes. Add a
clap on each hello. If the child is shy, let the stuffed animal sing first.

## 2. Body warm-up (4 min)

- Shake out hands like wet paintbrushes.
- Roll shoulders back three times.
- "Sleeping hands": curl fingers loosely, then open like a flower.
- Sit on the front half of the bench. Feet flat on the floor or a cushion.
- Place hands on the closed piano lid and make ten soft "raindrop" touches.
  Count them together.

## 3. High and low (5 min)

The stuffed animal walks on the keyboard. When the teacher plays high, the
animal jumps up; when the teacher plays low, it crouches down. Then the child
plays and the teacher moves the animal. Use big contrasts first, then smaller
ones.

## 4. Exploring the keys (7 min)

- Let the child play anywhere for 20 seconds. No instructions.
- Show the groups of two and three black keys. "Can you find a group of two?
  A group of three?"
- Walk one finger slowly up all the white keys and back down.
- Put the red sticker on middle C. "This is red C. It is our home key."
- Play red C together, softly. "The key is sleeping. Wake it gently."

## 5. Raindrops, storms, sunshine (7 min)

Make a tiny story with sounds:

- **Raindrops:** high, soft single notes.
- **Storm:** low, loud rumbles with the flat of the hand (not banging).
- **Sunshine:** a slow slide up the white keys.
- **Sleep:** one soft red C, then hands in lap.

The child chooses the order. The teacher narrates. This teaches loud/soft,
high/low, and gentle touch without the word "practice."

## 6. Play for the animal (2 min)

The child plays one raindrop and one storm for the stuffed animal. The animal
applauds by bouncing. Name one specific success: "Your raindrops were gentle
and your feet stayed still."

## 7. Home card (2 min)

> **Practice card 1 — 5 minutes, 5 days**
>
> 1. Ten raindrop touches on the closed lid.
> 2. Find a group of two black keys and a group of three.
> 3. Play red C three times, softly.
> 4. Sing the hello song.
>
> Grown-up: no correcting this week. Just play together.

## Teacher notes

- Do not introduce finger numbers or note names today. The goal is comfort.
- A child who will not touch the keys is not refusing: offer the stuffed
  animal's paw, or let the child play with one finger while holding a toy.
- Check bench height before the child sits. Most home benches are too low for
  a 4-year-old; a firm cushion under the child works.

## If the child races ahead

Show the two black-key groups and ask the child to find every C on the
keyboard. Put a second red sticker one octave up.

## If the child struggles

Shorten the exploring to two minutes and spend longer on the story. Ending
with one gentle sound is a success.
