# Lesson 3 — The Rainbow Keys

**Unit 1: Explore** · 30 minutes

**You need:** piano with color stickers C–G, color cards (printables page 3),
pom-pom or small ball, hand chart (page 4).

## I can…

- find C, D, E, F, and G by color;
- name my fingers 1 to 5;
- keep a bubble under my hand;
- play a three-note color echo.

## At a glance

| Min | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Hello song |
| 3–7 | Finger warm-up and the bubble |
| 7–12 | Rainbow key hunt |
| 12–19 | Finger numbers and five-finger position |
| 19–26 | Color echoes at the piano |
| 26–28 | Child's own color pattern |
| 28–30 | Home card |

## 1. Hello song (3 min)

Sing the hello song on G–E. Add finger wiggles: wiggle the finger that
matches each word. Keep it playful.

## 2. Finger warm-up and the bubble (4 min)

- Touch thumb to each fingertip in turn: 1-2, 1-3, 1-4, 1-5. Slow and careful.
- Place a pom-pom under the palm and rest the hand on the closed lid. The
  pom-pom should not escape. That curved shape is our piano hand.
- "Show me a claw. Now a flat pancake. Now a bubble." The bubble is the one we
  want.

## 3. Rainbow key hunt (5 min)

Review the sticker map on the keyboard.

- "Find red C." Child plays it.
- "Find blue G." Child plays it.
- Name a color, child finds the key. Then name the letter only.
- Speed round: three colors in ten seconds, but still gentle.

If the child is ready, point out that the colors repeat one octave higher:
"Red C has a twin up there."

## 4. Finger numbers (7 min)

Use the hand chart. Touch each finger as you say its number:

> 1 thumb, 2 pointer, 3 tall man, 4 ring, 5 pinky.

Place the right hand in five-finger position: thumb on red C, 2 on orange D,
3 on yellow E, 4 on green F, 5 on blue G. Check:

- curved fingers, not flat;
- thumb on its side;
- wrist level, not dropped;
- shoulders relaxed.

Play one note with each finger, saying the number aloud. Keep the other
fingers resting lightly on their keys.

## 5. Color echoes (7 min)

Teacher plays, child echoes. Start with two notes, then three.

```
red C – yellow E
red C – yellow E – blue G
blue G – yellow E – red C
red C – blue G – red C
```

Say the color and finger together: "red one, yellow three, blue five."

Then try a "walk": C D E F G, one finger at a time, saying "1, 2, 3, 4, 5."
The child does not need speed. A slow, even walk is the goal.

## 6. Child's color pattern (2 min)

The child chooses three color cards and plays them in that order. The teacher
echoes. Swap roles. Clap for the composer.

## 7. Home card (2 min)

> **Practice card 3 — 5 minutes, 5 days**
>
> 1. Bubble check: hold a pom-pom under the palm for ten seconds.
> 2. Rainbow hunt: find C, D, E, F, G by color, then by letter.
> 3. Finger walk: C D E F G with fingers 1 2 3 4 5, slow and even.
> 4. Play your three-color pattern for a grown-up.
>
> Grown-up: check the bubble, not the speed.

## Teacher notes

- Sticker the child's fingernails with numbers 1–5 if finger numbers keep
  slipping. Remove before the child leaves.
- The most common error is a collapsed pinky. Ask for a "tall pinky" rather
  than "curved finger."
- Do not add left hand yet. One hand at a time.

## If the child races ahead

Teach the left-hand mirror: pinky 5 on red C, thumb 1 on blue G. Play a
five-finger walk with the left hand.

## If the child struggles

Stay with three keys: C, E, G (fingers 1, 3, 5). Skip D and F this week. The
map can grow next time.
