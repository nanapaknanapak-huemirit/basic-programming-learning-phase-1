# Lesson 4 — Steady Beat and the Cuckoo

**Unit 1: Explore** · 30 minutes · End of Unit 1

**You need:** piano, rhythm sticks or spoons, color cards, cuckoo chart
(`song-charts/all-songs.md`, Tier 1).

## I can…

- clap walk and run-ning rhythms;
- keep a steady beat through a rest;
- play the cuckoo echo G–E;
- play with a steady beat.

## At a glance

| Min | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Hello song |
| 3–7 | March, clap, and freeze |
| 7–12 | Rainbow key review |
| 12–19 | Walk, run-ning, and shh |
| 19–26 | Cuckoo echoes at the piano |
| 26–28 | Rhythm telephone |
| 28–30 | Home card and Unit 1 check |

## 1. Hello song (3 min)

Sing the hello song. Today the child chooses loud or soft for the last
"hello." The teacher follows.

## 2. March, clap, and freeze (4 min)

March on "1, 2, 3, 4." Then clap the same beat while marching. Then clap
without marching, keeping the same speed. Freeze on the count of four, then
start again without losing the pulse.

## 3. Rainbow review (5 min)

Speed round: teacher says a color, child plays the key. Then letter names.
Then a three-note echo from Lesson 3. End with a slow five-finger walk,
C–G, saying finger numbers.

## 4. Walk, run-ning, and shh (7 min)

Use the words, not note values.

- **Walk:** one clap per beat. "1, 2, 3, 4."
- **Run-ning:** two claps in one beat. "run-ning, run-ning."
- **Shh:** hands on knees for one beat. Silence still counts.

Chant "Rain, rain, go a-way" with four walks. Then clap:

```
walk  walk  run-ning  walk   ("Rain, rain, go a-way")
walk  walk  run-ning  shh    ("come a-gain a-no-ther day")
```

Child claps, then teacher claps, then both. If the child loses the beat,
slow down and stomp the pulse with the feet.

## 5. Cuckoo echoes (7 min)

The cuckoo call is two notes: blue G, then yellow E.

1. Teacher sings "cuckoo" on G–E. Child sings back.
2. Teacher plays G–E. Child plays back with fingers 5 and 3.
3. Put the two notes on the beat: G on beat 1, E on beat 2, rest on 3–4.
4. Child plays "cuckoo" while the teacher claps the beat.
5. Add a second cuckoo: G E, rest, G E. Keep the rests alive by counting
   "shh, shh."

Use the cuckoo chart to show the two blue and yellow blocks. Point to each
block as the child plays.

## 6. Rhythm telephone (2 min)

Teacher claps a four-beat pattern using walk, run-ning, and shh. Child claps
it back. Three rounds, then swap. Keep patterns short enough to remember.

## 7. Home card and Unit 1 check (2 min)

> **Practice card 4 — 5 minutes, 5 days**
>
> 1. March and clap 1-2-3-4.
> 2. Clap: walk, walk, run-ning, walk.
> 3. Play the cuckoo: G–E, then rest for two beats.
> 4. Sing "Rain, rain, go away" with the claps.
>
> Grown-up: count out loud. Silence is part of the music.

**Unit 1 check.** The child should be able to find C–G by color, show a
curved hand, echo loud/soft, keep a beat for eight counts, and play G–E.
Mark it on `progress-tracker.md`. It is fine to repeat Lesson 4 before moving
on.

## Teacher notes

- The rest is the hardest part. Count it aloud and keep the hands moving
  slightly, or tap the knees.
- If the child plays G–E with a flat hand, return to the bubble and play the
  two notes with fingers 5 and 3 only.
- Some children need the cuckoo chart turned sideways or enlarged. Pointing
  with a finger while playing is allowed and helpful.

## If the child races ahead

Add a third cuckoo call and a "sleeping" rest of four beats. Or play G–E with
the left hand (1 on G, 3 on E) for a coordination challenge.

## If the child struggles

Drop the rhythm reading and keep only the cuckoo echo. Play G and E as two
big soft bounces, one per count, with no rest. Add the rest next week.
