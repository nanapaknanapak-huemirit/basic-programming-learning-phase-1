# Lesson 5 — Hot Cross Buns

**Unit 2: Colors Make Music** · 30 minutes · First full piece

**You need:** piano, Hot Cross Buns chart (printables page 8), color cards,
pencil.

## I can…

- follow a color chart from left to right;
- play E, D, C with fingers 3, 2, 1;
- keep the beat through a rest;
- play the whole of *Hot Cross Buns*.

## At a glance

| Min | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Hello song |
| 3–7 | 3-2-1 finger warm-up |
| 7–12 | Cuckoo and rhythm review |
| 12–19 | Reading the color chart |
| 19–26 | Building the song |
| 26–28 | Play for the animal |
| 28–30 | Home card |

## 1. Hello song (3 min)

Sing the hello song. Child chooses the tempo: fast, slow, or "sleepy."

## 2. 3-2-1 warm-up (4 min)

- Tap fingers 3-2-1 on the closed lid, then on the keys: E, D, C.
- Keep the bubble. The other fingers stay resting.
- Play E-D-C three times, slowly, saying "3, 2, 1."
- Check that the wrist is not dropping on the red C.

## 3. Review (5 min)

- Cuckoo echo G–E.
- Clap walk, walk, run-ning, walk.
- Rainbow hunt: find E, D, C by color, then by letter.
- Play a slow five-finger walk from C to G.

## 4. Reading the chart (7 min)

Show the Hot Cross Buns chart. This is the first time the child reads a whole
piece.

1. Point to the first block. "What color? What letter? Which finger?"
2. Sing the color names in rhythm: "yellow, orange, red, rest."
3. Point and sing the whole song before touching the keys.
4. Explain the rest: "The rest is a quiet beat. We count it, but we don't
   play."

Let the child trace the blocks with a finger while singing. Left to right,
like reading a book.

## 5. Building the song (7 min)

**Chunk 1:** E D C, rest. Play it twice. Then E D C, rest again.

**Chunk 2:** C C C C, then D D D D. Play with a steady beat. These are the
"one a penny, two a penny" notes.

**Chunk 3:** E D C, rest. This is the same as chunk 1.

**Put it together:** play the whole chart slowly. If the child loses the
place, point to the current block. Repeat three times:

- once medium,
- once very soft (mouse),
- once a little faster (but still even).

Do not play it more than five times in a row. Stand up and shake between
tries.

## 6. Play for the animal (2 min)

The stuffed animal is the audience. The child bows, plays, and bows again.
Name one specific thing: "Your rest was completely silent — that's hard!"

## 7. Home card (2 min)

> **Practice card 5 — 5 minutes, 5 days**
>
> 1. 3-2-1 taps: E D C, three times.
> 2. Sing the colors: yellow, orange, red, rest.
> 3. Play *Hot Cross Buns* twice. The second time can be soft.
> 4. Play it for a grown-up and take a bow.
>
> Grown-up: point to the next color only if the child loses the place.

## Teacher notes

- The most common error is rushing the C C C C / D D D D section. Count
  aloud or tap the beat on the child's shoulder.
- If the child reads the letters but not the colors, cover the letters with a
  finger. If the child reads colors but not letters, say both together.
- Keep the hand in C position. Do not let the thumb drift.

## If the child races ahead

Ask for a version with a different ending: E D C, then C C C (three soft
notes). Or play the whole song with the left hand (5-4-3).

## If the child struggles

Learn only the first phrase this week: E D C, rest. Put a sticker on the
chart at the end of the phrase. The rest of the song can wait.
