# Lesson 8 — Color Chart Challenge

**Unit 2: Colors Make Music** · 30 minutes · End of Unit 2

**You need:** piano, all charts so far, color cards, compose sheet
(printables page 13), pencil, phone or tablet for recording.

## I can…

- perform one song for a listener;
- compose a short color pattern;
- play my pattern with a steady beat;
- listen to my playing and say what I like.

## At a glance

| Min | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Hello song |
| 3–7 | Rainbow run warm-up |
| 7–12 | Mystery song review |
| 12–19 | Composing with color cards |
| 19–26 | Perform, record, and listen |
| 26–28 | Concert and applause |
| 28–30 | Home card and Unit 2 check |

## 1. Hello song (3 min)

Sing the hello song. Child chooses one song from the last three weeks to play
immediately, while the teacher sings along.

## 2. Rainbow run (4 min)

Play C D E F G up and back down, slow and even. Then try it with eyes closed.
Then play only the colors the teacher calls: "red, green, blue, orange,
yellow." This is a warm-up and a map check at the same time.

## 3. Mystery song (5 min)

Teacher plays the first three notes of a known song and the child guesses:

- E D C … *Hot Cross Buns*.
- E D C D … *Mary Had a Little Lamb*.
- G E … the cuckoo.

Then swap: child plays a mystery and teacher guesses. This builds memory and
listening, and it is funny when the teacher guesses wrong.

## 4. Composing (7 min)

1. Choose four color cards. Put them in a row.
2. Clap the pattern, one clap per card: "yellow, orange, red, blue."
3. Play it on the keys. If a note feels wrong, change the card. There are no
   wrong notes, only choices.
4. Add a rhythm: all walks, or one run-ning pair, or a rest at the end.
5. Write the colors in the first four boxes of the compose sheet.
6. If the child wants more, add four more boxes. Repeat the first four at the
   end so the song sounds finished: first four … first four again.

Composition rules for the teacher: the child chooses; the teacher only asks
"what happens next?" Do not fix the melody to sound like a familiar tune.

## 5. Perform and listen (7 min)

- The child performs the composition, then one review song.
- Record 20 seconds on a phone.
- Listen back together. Ask: "What did you like? What would you change?"
- Accept any answer. "I liked the red part" is a musical observation.

Recording is optional. If the child dislikes hearing themselves, skip it and
describe the performance instead.

## 6. Concert (2 min)

Set up two chairs for an audience of stuffed animals. The child bows, plays,
and bows. Teacher claps loudly. Name one specific success and one tiny next
step.

## 7. Home card and Unit 2 check (2 min)

> **Practice card 8 — 5 minutes, 5 days**
>
> 1. Rainbow run: C D E F G and back.
> 2. Play your new composition three times.
> 3. Play your favorite song from Unit 2.
> 4. Choose the song you might play at the recital.
>
> Grown-up: ask "what happens next?" instead of "play it this way."

**Unit 2 check.** The child can play *Hot Cross Buns* and *Mary Had a Little
Lamb*, follow a color chart left to right, keep a beat through a rest, and
invent a short pattern. Mark `progress-tracker.md`.

## Teacher notes

- Some children freeze when asked to compose. Start by laying out three cards
  and asking "which one first?" Choice is composition.
- Do not correct the composition's tune. Correct only the beat and the hand
  shape.
- If the child wants to perform the same song for the recital, that is the
  right choice. Familiar is confident.

## If the child races ahead

Add a left-hand red C drone on beat 1 of the composition. Or compose a "question
and answer": four beats high, four beats low.

## If the child struggles

Compose with only two cards. Play them in different orders and choose a
favorite. Four beats is a complete song at this age.
