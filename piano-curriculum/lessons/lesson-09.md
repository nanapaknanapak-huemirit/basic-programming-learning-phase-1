# Lesson 9 — From Colors to Lines

**Unit 3: Colors Become Notes** · 30 minutes

**You need:** piano, staff sheet (printables page 14), Twinkle chart
(page 10), color cards, pencil.

## I can…

- point to a line and a space on the staff;
- match a colored notehead to its key;
- play the first phrase of *Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star*;
- keep the beat while I read.

## At a glance

| Min | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Hello song |
| 3–7 | Five-line hand game |
| 7–12 | Staff tour |
| 12–19 | Matching staff notes to keys |
| 19–26 | Twinkle, phrase 1 |
| 26–28 | Line or space? |
| 28–30 | Home card |

## 1. Hello song (3 min)

Sing the hello song. Today, hum the last line instead of singing it. Child
copies.

## 2. Five-line hand game (4 min)

Hold up one hand, fingers spread. The five fingers are the five lines of the
staff.

- Touch the fingertips: "line, line, line."
- Touch the gaps between: "space, space, space."
- Chant: "line, space, line, space" while touching finger, gap, finger, gap.

This gives the child the physical shape of the staff before seeing it on
paper.

## 3. Staff tour (5 min)

Show the staff sheet.

- Five lines, four spaces. Count them.
- The curly symbol at the start is the treble clef.
- Point to the bottom line: "This line is E."
- Point to the space just below the staff: "This space is D."
- Point to the short line below the staff: "This is C's own little line. It is
  called a ledger line."

Do not drill "Every Good Boy Deserves Fudge" yet. The colored noteheads are
the bridge. Say **"blue diamond G"** and point to the second line.

## 4. Staff to key (7 min)

Use the top staff on page 14, where the notes are colored.

1. Teacher points to C. Child says "red C" and plays it.
2. Repeat for D, E, F, G, A.
3. Teacher points to two notes. Child plays them in order.
4. Child points, teacher plays. Swap roles.

Then cover the letters at the bottom of the staff with a strip of paper. Can
the child still find the notes by color and position? If yes, the bridge is
working.

## 5. Twinkle, phrase 1 (7 min)

The first phrase is C C G G A A G.

1. Sing the words: "Twin-kle, twin-kle, lit-tle star."
2. Play it from the color chart (page 10).
3. Show the same notes on the staff. Point to each notehead as the child
   plays.
4. The A is purple, one step above blue G. Reach with the pinky, or slide the
   whole hand up one key if the stretch is uncomfortable.
5. Play phrase 1 three times: once with the chart, once with the staff, once
   from memory.

Do not add phrase 2 today unless the child is eager. One secure phrase is
better than a shaky song.

## 6. Line or space? (2 min)

Teacher points to a note on the staff. Child says "line" or "space," then
plays it. Three rounds. Keep it quick and game-like.

## 7. Home card (2 min)

> **Practice card 9 — 5 minutes, 5 days**
>
> 1. Show five lines and four spaces on your hand.
> 2. Point to C, D, E, F, G, A on the staff sheet.
> 3. Play Twinkle phrase 1 three times.
> 4. Sing "line, space, line, space."
>
> Grown-up: ask "is it on a line or in a space?" before asking for the letter.

## Teacher notes

- The colored notehead is doing the work this week. The letter and position
  are backup cues. Do not remove the colors yet.
- If the child confuses the space below the staff with the first space, put a
  small dot on the D space. One landmark is enough.
- Some children read the staff vertically (top to bottom) instead of left to
  right. Point along the staff as they play.

## If the child races ahead

Teach phrase 2 (F F E E D D C) on the staff. Point out that it moves down by
step, one line or space at a time.

## If the child struggles

Stay on the keyboard and the color cards. Put the staff sheet on the wall and
simply name "line" and "space" this week. The staff will still be there next
lesson.
