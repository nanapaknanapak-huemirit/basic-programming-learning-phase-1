# Lesson 11 — Two Hands

**Unit 3: Colors Become Notes** · 30 minutes

**You need:** piano, Jingle Bells chart (printables page 12), Ode chart,
letter-only sequence for Jingle (from `song-charts/all-songs.md`), white paper
strips or sticky notes.

## I can…

- hold a left-hand C for four beats;
- play the Jingle Bells chorus with my right hand;
- put a left-hand drone under a melody;
- play some notes without looking at the color.

## At a glance

| Min | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Hello song |
| 3–7 | Rock and bird warm-up |
| 7–12 | Ode review |
| 12–19 | Building the drone |
| 19–26 | Jingle Bells, two hands |
| 26–28 | Sleigh ride game |
| 28–30 | Home card |

## 1. Hello song (3 min)

Sing the hello song. Today, hold one long note on the piano through the whole
song. Child chooses the note, teacher holds it. Listen to how the long note
and the melody fit together.

## 2. Rock and bird (4 min)

- **Rock:** left-hand pinky (5) on red C. Press and hold for four counts.
  Keep the wrist soft. Release.
- **Bird:** right hand plays C D E F G while the rock holds.
- Swap: left hand plays the five-finger walk, right hand holds C.

If holding four beats is uncomfortable, hold for two, or play the C on beat 1
and release. A relaxed short drone is better than a tense long one.

## 3. Ode review (5 min)

Play the Ode phrase from last week. Then play it with a left-hand C on beat 1
of each four-beat group. The left hand should land softly, like a heartbeat.

## 4. Building the drone (7 min)

A **drone** is a note that stays the same while the melody moves.

1. Left hand alone: C – – – (hold four), then lift. Repeat four times.
2. Right hand alone: play the first phrase of Jingle, "Jingle bells, jingle
   bells."
3. Together: left hand holds C for four beats while the right hand plays.
   Start with only one bar together, then two.

If the child cannot hold and play at the same time, the teacher can hold the
drone while the child plays the melody, then swap. The child still hears the
texture.

## 5. Jingle Bells, two hands (7 min)

Learn the right hand first, in two chunks.

**Chunk A:** E E E, rest, E E E, rest, E G C D, E (hold).

**Chunk B:** F F F F, F E E E, E D D E, D, G.

Then add the left-hand C drone under chunk A. If that works, add it under
chunk B as well. Keep the left hand on one key; do not move it.

**Color fading.** Cover two or three colored blocks with a white paper strip
and write the letters on the strip. The child plays those notes from the
letter only. Move the strips each repetition. By lesson 12, cover more.

## 6. Sleigh ride (2 min)

Play the Jingle chorus at three tempos: walking, trotting, and sleigh ride.
The child chooses. Keep the left-hand drone steady at every tempo. Stop
suddenly on "whoa!" and freeze.

## 7. Home card (2 min)

> **Practice card 11 — 5 minutes, 5 days**
>
> 1. Rock: hold left-hand C for four beats, four times.
> 2. Right hand: Jingle chunk A.
> 3. Two hands: drone + chunk A, slowly.
> 4. Play Ode with a C heartbeat on beat 1.
>
> Grown-up: if the left hand tightens, stop and shake it out. Relaxed and
> short beats tense and long.

## Teacher notes

- Left-hand pinky power is limited at this age. Do not demand a four-beat
  hold if the joint collapses. Two beats is fine.
- The drone can be played on beat 1 only. It still teaches hand independence.
- Keep the color strips as a game, not a test. If the child protests, put the
  color back and try again next week.

## If the child races ahead

Move the drone to G for a "sparkle" version, or alternate C and G on beats 1
and 3. Add a soft ending: hold both C's together.

## If the child struggles

Keep the drone in the teacher's hand and let the child play the melody. Or
play the melody with the right hand and tap the drone rhythm on the closed
lid. The coordination can be built away from the keys.
