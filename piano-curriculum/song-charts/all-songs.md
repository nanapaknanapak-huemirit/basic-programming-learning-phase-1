# Song Charts

All songs use the home octave C–A. The child's right hand stays in C
position (thumb on C, pinky on G) unless a chart says otherwise.

**How to read a chart**

- Read left to right, one block at a time.
- The color tells you which key. The letter inside is the backup cue.
- `–` means hold the note for two beats.
- A space is a short rest; breathe and keep the beat going.

Colored, ready-to-print versions are in
`printables/piano-printables.html`, pages 8–12.

---

## Tier 1 — Two and three notes

### 1. Cuckoo Echo

Two notes, G and E. Teacher sings "cuckoo," child answers.

```
blue  yellow   |  blue  yellow
 G      E      |   G      E
cu     ckoo    |  cu     ckoo
```

Letter-only: `G E | G E`

Teaching tip: play it as an echo first, then point at the chart. Ask the child
to keep the blue key down for two beats at the end ("the cuckoo is yawning").

---

### 2. Hot Cross Buns

Three notes, E D C, fingers 3–2–1. This is the first full piece.

```
yellow orange red   (rest)  |  yellow orange red   (rest)
  E      D     C              E      D     C
 Hot   cross  buns           Hot   cross  buns

red   red   red   red   |  orange orange orange orange
 C     C     C     C         D      D      D      D
one    a    pen   ny       two     a     pen    ny

yellow orange red   (rest)
  E      D     C
 Hot   cross  buns
```

Letter-only:
`E D C – | E D C – | C C C C | D D D D | E D C –`

Teaching tip: sing the color names in rhythm before playing. The repeated
C and D measures are the easiest place to lose the beat, so count aloud.

---

### 3. Color Echo Patterns (original)

Teacher plays, child echoes. Use any of these four-beat patterns.

```
red   yellow blue   red     C E G C
blue  yellow red    blue    G E C G
red   red   blue    blue    C C G G
yellow orange red   –       E D C –
```

Letter-only: `C E G C` · `G E C G` · `C C G G` · `E D C –`

Teaching tip: start with two notes, add the third when the echo is secure.
This is improvisation practice disguised as a game.

## Tier 2 — Five-finger melodies

### 4. Mary Had a Little Lamb

Notes used: E D C D E G. Fingers 3–2–1–2–3–5.

```
yellow orange red orange | yellow yellow yellow  (rest)
  E      D     C    D         E      E      E
 Ma     ry    had   a        lit    tle    lamb

orange orange orange  (rest) | yellow blue blue  (rest)
  D      D      D              E     G    G
lit    tle    lamb            Ma    ry   had

yellow orange red orange | yellow yellow yellow yellow
  E      D     C    D         E      E      E      E
lit    tle    lamb, its     fleece  was   white  as

orange orange yellow orange | red   –
  D      D      E      D        C
 snow   snow   as     snow     (hold)
```

Letter-only:
`E D C D E E E | D D D | E G G | E D C D E E E E | D D E D C –`

Teaching tip: the leap E–G–G in phrase 3 is the only skip. Circle it in blue
and practise it alone three times before playing the whole song.

---

### 5. Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star

Notes used: C D E F G A. Fingers 1–2–3–4–5 for C–G; A is played with
finger 5 after a small reach, or with the hand shifted up one key.

```
red red blue blue | purple purple blue –
 C   C    G   G       A      A     G
Twin-kle twin-kle    lit-  tle   star

green green yellow yellow | orange orange red –
  F     F     E      E         D      D     C
how   I    won-   der        what   you  are

blue blue green green | yellow yellow orange –
 G    G     F     F       E      E      D
Up   a-   bove  the      world  so    high

blue blue green green | yellow yellow orange –
 G    G     F     F       E      E      D
like  a    dia-  mond     in    the    sky

red red blue blue | purple purple blue –
 C   C    G   G       A      A     G
Twin-kle twin-kle    lit-  tle   star

green green yellow yellow | orange orange red –
  F     F     E      E         D      D     C
how   I    won-   der        what   you  are
```

Letter-only:
`C C G G A A G – | F F E E D D C – | G G F F E E D – | G G F F E E D – | C C G G A A G – | F F E E D D C –`

Teaching tip: this is the first song with a repeated phrase. Let the child
notice that phrases 3 and 4 are identical. Use a highlighter on the chart.

## Tier 3 — Longer melodies and two hands

### 6. Ode to Joy (first phrase)

Notes used: C D E F G. Stay in C position.

```
yellow yellow green blue | blue green yellow orange
  E      E      F    G       G     F      E      D

red red orange yellow | yellow orange orange –
 C   C    D      E         E      D      D
```

Letter-only:
`E E F G | G F E D | C C D E | E D D –`

Then repeat the whole phrase once more, ending on C:
`E E F G | G F E D | C C D E | D C C –`

Teaching tip: sing it on "da" first. The shape is a wave: up four notes, down
four notes. Draw the wave in the air together.

---

### 7. Jingle Bells (chorus)

Notes used: C D E F G. First half uses fingers 1–5; the F–F–F run uses finger
4 repeatedly.

```
yellow yellow yellow – | yellow yellow yellow –
  E      E      E          E      E      E
Jin-   gle    bells      jin-   gle    bells

yellow blue red orange | yellow – – –
  E     G    C    D        E
jin-  gle  all  the      way!

green green green green | green yellow yellow yellow
  F     F     F     F       F     E      E      E
Oh    what  fun   it      is    to     ride   in

yellow orange orange yellow | orange – blue –
  E      D      D      E         D      G
a      one-   horse  o-        pen    sleigh
```

Letter-only:
`E E E – | E E E – | E G C D | E – – – | F F F F | F E E E | E D D E | D – G –`

Teaching tip: the first "E – – –" is a whole note. Let the child lift the hand
high on beat four to prepare the next phrase. For a two-hand version, hold a
red C drone in the left hand through the whole chorus (see Lesson 11).

## Rhythm chants (no pitch)

Use these when the child needs a movement break.

| Chant | Say | Clap |
|---|---|---|
| Rain, rain | "Rain, rain, go a-way" | walk walk walk walk |
| Bee, bee | "Bee, bee, bum-ble-bee" | walk walk run-ning walk |
| Sleepy bear | "Sleep-ing bear, sleep-ing bear" | hold hold walk walk |

Count "1, 2, 3, 4" and keep the pulse in the feet.
