that file once and reuse the pages below. This index explains what each sheet
is for, the answer key, and what to say while the child works.

## Answer keys

**Color Hunt (page 5).** C red, D orange, E yellow, F green, G blue, A
purple, B pink, the upper C red again. The second C is the same color as the
first: say "C has a twin."

**Steady beat (page 5).** There is no written answer. The child taps eight
even beats, then claps the word pattern:

```
walk  walk  run-ning  walk  run-ning  walk
```

**Hot Cross Buns (page 8).** E D C – | E D C – | C C C C | D D D D | E D C –.
Fingers 3 2 1, then 1 1 1 1 and 2 2 2 2.

**Mary Had a Little Lamb (page 9).** E D C D E E E | D D D | E G G | E D C D
E E E E | D D E D C. The only skip is E up to G.

**Twinkle (page 10).** C C G G A A G | F F E E D D C | G G F F E E D | G G F
F E E D | C C G G A A G | F F E E D D C. Phrases 3 and 4 are identical.

**Ode to Joy (page 11).** E E F G | G F E D | C C D E | E D D –, then repeat
and end D C C –. No skips: all steps and repeated notes.

**Jingle Bells (page 12).** E E E – | E E E – | E G C D | E – – – | F F F F |
F E E E | E D D E | D – G –.

**Staff sheet (page 14).** Bottom line E, first space F, second line G,
second space A, third line B, third space C, fourth line D, fourth space E,
top line F. C sits on a ledger line below the staff; D sits in the space just
below the staff. For this curriculum the child only needs C–A.

**Compose sheet (page 13).** No wrong answers. A finished-sounding song
usually ends on red C. Check the beat, not the tune.

## How to use the sheets

- **Point, don't correct.** Point to the next block or notehead. Let the child
  find it.
- **One sheet at a time.** Put the others away. A cluttered music stand is a
  distraction.
- **Write on them.** Circle phrase starts, draw arrows for skips, or cover a
  color with a white sticker to fade the color cue.
- **Keep them.** A folder of completed sheets is a visible record of progress
  and a good recital audience handout.
